## Star Ladder

The implemented star ladder is:

- 0 stars: Not yet correct
- 1 star: Got right once
- 2 stars: Got right twice
- 3 stars: Got right 3 times
- 4 stars: Got right 4 times
- 5 stars: Got right 5 times

The UI does not call 5 stars mastered.

## Target Family Denominators

Category denominators are derived from target families/practice targets, using the existing target-family metadata where present and conservative fallback metadata where necessary.

Repeated variants and repeated attempts can increase a target's star level, but they do not increase the category denominator.

## Correct / Incorrect / Guessed Handling

- Correct auto-graded, non-assisted attempts increment the target correct count up to the five-star cap.
- Guessed correct answers may count towards the star ladder, while the confidence context remains available in the target/debug data.
- Incorrect answers increase the target/category recent-wrong signal and review priority, but do not demote stars.

## Assisted / Teach Me Handling

Teach Me, revealed, hinted, or assisted attempts do not count as normal correct star credit. The path calculator excludes:

- `result:"assisted"`
- `supportLevel:"teach_me_assisted"`
- `hintUsed:true`

These attempts remain useful evidence elsewhere but do not create normal repeated-correct stars.

## Production Handling

Production cards remain self-marked practice evidence only.

- `I practised this` style production evidence increments practised counts.
- Production does not create correctness stars.
- Production does not alter scoring.
- Production can satisfy production-practised breadth where production exists in a category, without implying correctness.

## Task-Type Breadth Handling

Recommended continuation requires:

- at least 50% of current category core targets at one star or more;
- no missing required task types;
- production practised at least once where production is present/required.

The browser smoke created synthetic breadth evidence and verified that a category can become recommended only after both the 50% threshold and task-type breadth were satisfied.

## Soft Unlock Handling

Locked categories show a soft unlock dialogue:

- `Keep practising` returns the learner to the previous available category without writing an override.
- `Unlock anyway` records additive override metadata under `ozmosis_b1_path_unlock_overrides_v1`.

Manual override does not mark any previous category complete.
