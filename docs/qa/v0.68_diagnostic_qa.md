## 4. Diagnostic QA Scope

Audited possible patterns, recent error types, connector diagnostic cards, repair candidates, repair queues, targeted repair, Suggested Next Practice route hints, debug metadata, and copy safety.

## 5. Overdiagnosis Audit

One wrong answer, thin evidence, diagnostic-only evidence, self-marked production-only evidence, and scaffolded-only repeated evidence do not create learner-facing possible-pattern or connector diagnostic cards.

## 6. Threshold Audit

Possible patterns and connector cards require at least three relevant attempts and at least two similar errors. Repair queues require a routeable candidate, exact route fit, and at least four matching existing items.

## 7. Learner Copy Audit

Trouble/diagnosis-style copy was tightened to "pattern", "area to recheck", and "learner label" wording.

## 8. Route Consistency Audit

Debug QA summary validates route hints against the existing route set.

## 9. Evidence Recording Audit

Diagnostic cards, candidates, and queue previews remain read-time summaries and do not record evidence.

## 10. Production Evidence Audit

Self-marked production-only evidence remains unable to create routeable diagnostic/repair claims.

## 11. Diagnostic Evidence Audit

Diagnostic-only evidence remains snapshot/context evidence and cannot become routeable by itself.

## 12. Debug Privacy Audit

Debug QA summary exposes counts, route hints, IDs, limitations, and suppression reasons rather than full learner answer text by default.

## 13. Learning Map Density Audit

Default cards remain compact; details stay behind Explain/Why.

## 14. Suggested Next Practice Audit

Suggested Next Practice remains conservative and routes to targeted repair only when a high-quality queue exists.
