## 5. Why v0.85.3.2 Cloze Proof Was Insufficient

The v0.85.3.2 cloze proof used the staged fixture `Niveau ___.`

That proved geometry could be forced into the target area, but it did not prove a natural B1 sentence-like cloze such as `Welche ___ soll ich mitbringen?`.

- sentence zone starts around `y 110`;

- pre-answer star/status remains absent;
- HUD does not cover input or controls.

## 7. Representative Cloze Fixtures

QA-only fixtures used:

- Short inline: `Welche ___ soll ich mitbringen?` / `Unterlagen`.
- With cue: `Ich lerne Deutsch auf ___.` / cue `Sie nennen Ihr Sprachniveau.` / `B1-Niveau`.
- Long/wrapping: `Ich möchte den Termin auf ___ verschieben.` / `nächsten Montag`.

## 8. QA-Only Fixture Policy

Fixtures were staged only through `window.ozmosisB1Debug().qaStageItemById(...)` inside the browser QA harness.

They were not added to normal practice, content banks, scoring, storage, or route composition.

## 9. Cloze Renderer Changes

Runtime changes were narrow:

- `b1ClozeWidthStyle(...)` now derives an inline cloze width from answer length unless a QA-only width override is supplied.
- Typed cloze CSS now respects `--clozeWidth` instead of forcing every blank to `258px`.
- The hidden typed-cloze instruction line no longer leaves its `<br>` behind, so the sentence starts in the intended prompt zone.

Answer checking was not changed.

## 10. Cue Placement Decision

Decision: cue below the cloze sentence in the existing secondary support/cue line.

Reason: this preserves the ScreenConcept sentence zone and prevents under-context cues from pushing the cloze sentence too low.

Measured cue fixture:

- sentence `y 110`;
- cue block `y 180.6`;
- cue remains LTR and secondary.
