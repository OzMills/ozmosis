- Exact `variantId` is recorded.
- `siblingVariantGroup`, `reviewGroup`, `familyType`, `familyTarget`, and `sameSessionEvidenceCap` are recorded where available.

- No clean `Umformen` variant was imported.

## 11. Known Limitations

- Same-family evidence is metadata-tagged as sibling evidence, but no new durability/scheduler/review semantics were activated.
- Correct-with-note remains limited to current app support; B1-01 exact-only import avoids unsafe fallback correctness for strong evidence.
- The B1-01 variants are static approved variants, not a general grammar generator.
- B1-01 is reachable from the Grammar hub; it was not added to Home to avoid broader IA/route redesign.
- Some temporary Edge CDP profile folders from failed harness attempts remained under `_development-files` because Windows denied deletion on locked `EdgeEDropSQLite.db-journal` files. They were left untouched and are not source/content/QA files.
