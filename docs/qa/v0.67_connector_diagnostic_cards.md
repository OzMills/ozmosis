## 4. Connector Diagnostic Implementation

Added `b1ConnectorDiagnosticCards(...)`, a derived read-time signal layer for repeated connector meaning, connector word-order, and connector-position evidence. Cards appear in the Learning Map only when thresholds pass.

## 5. Supported Connector Families

- Meaning confusion.
- Syntax / word order.
- Connector position / verb placement.

## 6. Deferred Connector Families

- Register mismatch is deferred because current metadata is not explicit enough.

## 7. Threshold Handling

Cards require at least three relevant connector attempts and at least two similar errors. No-evidence, one-wrong, and two-attempt fixtures were suppressed.

## 8. Meaning Confusion Handling

Repeated connector-choice evidence can produce a `Connector meaning` card with a Connector Sprint route hint.

## 9. Syntax / Word Order Handling

Repeated word-order cloze or repair evidence can produce a `Connector word order` card with a Grammar repair route hint.

## 10. Register Handling

Register mismatch remains deferred.
