## 10. Daily Sprint Bank-Inclusion Audit

Current v0.38 behaviour:

- Included in Today’s B1 Sprint:
  - `core`
  - `redemittel` non-production items
  - `wortschatz` non-production mapped items
- Not included in Today’s B1 Sprint:
  - extracted `connectors` bank
  - Redemittel production cards
  - Wortschatz production cards

Runtime evidence:

- Today’s B1 Sprint opened in mode `mixed`.
- Sampled queue types included `redemittel_cloze`, `phrase_choice`, `error_repair`, `vocab_cloze`, and `grammar_cloze`.
- Sampled queue did not include Connector Sprint item types.

Policy result: AMBIGUOUS, NOT BLOCKING.

The v0.38 registry makes the current policy explicit with `connectors.includeInDaily: false`. README/source brief do not clearly require Connector Sprint items inside Daily Sprint. Older changelog language around connector extraction mentions preserving Today’s B1 Sprint inclusion, which could be read as an expectation. Recommendation: decide separately whether Connector Sprint should join Daily Sprint in v0.40 or a focused v0.39.1 policy patch. No behaviour was changed in this QA pass.
