## 7. Task-Screen Results

- Normal B1 task screens now render compact medal stars via `b1PathStarGlyphsHtml(...)`.
- The visible strip uses concise copy such as `Got right twice`.
- The visible strip no longer contains `Practice progress`, category IDs such as `B1.15`, or task-type labels such as `Cloze`.
- Stars expose accessible labels such as `2 of 5 stars. Got right twice.`

## 8. Post-Answer Results

- Correct feedback shows medal stars plus concise copy such as `Now got right 3 times.`
- Wrong feedback shows `No new star. This will return for practice.`
- Assisted feedback shows `Practised with help. Stars need an unaided correct answer.`
- Diagnostic feedback remains snapshot-only.
- Production feedback shows `Production practised.` and `Not star-rated.`

## 9. Home / Landing Results

## 11. Accessibility Results

- Compact star glyphs use `role="img"` and an `aria-label`.
- B1 Path bars use `role="img"` and a text label that reports counts by star tier.
- Earned/unearned states are not communicated by colour alone.

## 12. Star Logic Preservation

- Stars still increment only from unassisted correct auto-graded evidence.
- Assisted, hinted, revealed, and Teach Me evidence does not increment stars.
- Diagnostic evidence does not increment stars.
- Production remains self-marked/practised-only and not correctness-starred.
- The star cap remains five.
