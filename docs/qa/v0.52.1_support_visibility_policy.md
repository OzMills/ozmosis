## 4. Current Support Rendering Before Patch

B1 support text was rendered from `support_en` / mapped `translation_en`. For B1 items, available support was generally shown before the answer unless the global support option was off.

## 5. Support Visibility Policy

Added conservative item-level policy helpers:

- `b1CanonicalSupportVisibility(...)`
- `b1SupportVisibilityPolicy(item, context)`
- `b1ShouldShowSupportBeforeAnswer(item, context)`
- `b1ShouldShowSupportAfterAnswer(item, context)`

Supported policy values:

- `visible`
- `collapsed`
- `after_answer`
- `hidden`
- `unknown`

Unknown or missing policy preserves default visible behavior.

## 7. Support-Level Interaction

Edge/CDP verified:

- After-answer connector support did not count as support used before the answer; evidence recorded `supportLevel = "unassisted_choice"`.
- Collapsed Redemittel support did not show until opened; after opening, evidence recorded `supportLevel = "translation_visible"`.
- Visible Wortschatz/default support showed before answer and recorded `supportLevel = "translation_visible"`.

## 8. Connector / Temporal Contrast Audit

The narrow implemented connector sample is `obwohl` vs `trotzdem`, where English support can reveal the target meaning too directly.

Broader future audit candidates:

- `weil` / `denn` / `deshalb`
- `damit` / `um ... zu`
- `wenn` / `als`
- `bevor` / `nachdem`
- `während` connector vs preposition-like use

No broad connector metadata rewrite was performed.

## 9. Redemittel Support Audit

The narrow implemented Redemittel sample is formal closing support for `Mit freundlichen Grüßen`, collapsed behind a neutral `Support` control.

Broader future audit candidates:

- polite request choices where English says "polite";
- formal/informal distractor sets where English wording gives away register;
- complaint/request items whose support text names the target phrase.

No broad Redemittel metadata rewrite was performed.
