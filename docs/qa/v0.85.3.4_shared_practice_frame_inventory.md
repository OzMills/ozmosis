## 9. Divergences Found

The answer to the core question is no.

Major divergences:

- Article/case table is owned by `renderMiniTable(mode)` in the legacy Case Trainer path, not by the B1 display adapter layer.
- B1 typed repair and Case Trainer typed article answers both use `#answerInput`, but B1 uses `b1Runtime` / `checkB1Answer()` / `renderB1Feedback()` while Case Trainer uses `current` / `checkAnswer()` / `renderMiniTable()`.
- B1 result flow and Case Trainer result flow use different summary/routing models.
- B1 cases/articles repair items do not currently show the protected article grid after wrong case/article answers.

## 12. Article Grid Audit

Found the protected article grid in Case Trainer:

- full/reveal grid: `renderMiniTable("hint")`
- correct highlight grid: `renderMiniTable("correct")`
- wrong-answer highlight grid: `renderMiniTable("missed")`
- classes: `.miniTable`, `.target`, `.hitCorrect`, `.hitMissed`

Static audit also found article/case result/heatmap behaviour through `showResults()`, `weakSpotHtml()`, and `resultHeatmapType`.

## 13. Article Grid Preservation Plan

The article grid must remain protected in three forms:

- full article grid for Article Tester / Case Trainer, result, and review screens;
- compact highlighted article grid for wrong article/case feedback;
- optional reveal article grid for `Show article grid`, teaching mode, or after-answer support.

Do not flatten the grid into generic feedback text.

## 14. Results Screen Considerations

B1 results and Case Trainer results are separate today. B1 results use evidence/session language and route back to Learning Map or the route hub. Case Trainer results use score, rank, and heatmap language.

The next convergence patch should define a common result-frame slot without changing scoring or evidence semantics.

## 15. Wrong-Article Feedback Grid Plan

Wrong article/case feedback should use a compact highlighted grid that can identify:

- case;
- gender;
- number;
- article family;
- correct cell;
- wrong selected cell where applicable.

The full grid should not be shown before answer unless teaching/support mode is explicitly active.
