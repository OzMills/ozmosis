## 5. Current Recommendation Logic Before Patch

v0.56 already considered task-depth gaps, repair evidence, contrast evidence, support-level context, and production self-marking limits. The risk was inflation: once global evidence reached a small threshold, row-level candidates could still be influenced by diagnostic-only, self-marked, scaffolded, recognition-only, or broad-category evidence.

## 6. Evidence Inflation Risks Found

- Row candidates did not require enough usable non-diagnostic, non-production evidence.
- Recognition-only evidence could contribute to repair/word-order gap pressure.
- Scaffolded-majority evidence could reach ordinary task-gap candidates instead of staying a support-context issue.
- Diagnostic-only and production-only records needed explicit fallback handling.
- Broad Wortschatz evidence needed extra caution because a few items do not represent the whole bucket.

## 7. Thin Evidence Handling

Global empty/thin evidence still recommends the Diagnostic Sprint. Thin fixtures with two records rendered the recommendation card, kept route `diagnostic`, and carried `thin_sample` metadata.

## 8. Easy-Task Inflation Handling

Recognition-only evidence is flagged as `recognition_only` when it would otherwise point toward repair, contrast, production, or sentence-building. Ordinary repair/contrast candidates now require at least three usable row records and at least some non-recognition recall evidence where relevant.

## 9. Scaffolded Evidence Handling

Scaffolded-majority row evidence no longer reaches ordinary task-gap or caution-signal candidates. The rendered scaffolded fixture used `support_context_gap`, routed to an existing closest route, and exposed `scaffolded_majority`.

## 10. Production Evidence Handling

Production-only evidence falls back to `production_self_marked_only` with copy asking for auto-checked practice evidence. Production remains self-marked practice evidence only.

## 11. Diagnostic Evidence Handling

Diagnostic-only evidence falls back to `diagnostic_only` with copy asking for ordinary practice evidence before reading task gaps strongly. It does not produce repair, contrast, or ordinary under-tested task recommendations.

## 12. Repair / Contrast Recommendation Handling

Repair and contrast recommendations still render when usable evidence supports them:

- Repair-gap fixture: `task_depth_repair_gap`, route `case_trainer`, task depth `repair`.
- Contrast-gap fixture: `task_depth_contrast_gap`, route `grammar`, task depth `contrast`.

Both used closest-route wording where the exact task route was unavailable.

## 13. Broad Category / Wortschatz Handling

Sparse Wortschatz cloze evidence no longer pressures repair/contrast/sentence-building gaps. The broad Wortschatz fixture rendered `under_tested_task_type`, route `wortschatz`, task depth `recognition`, and visible copy included that Wortschatz is broad and this is only a small next step.

## 14. Route Fallback Handling

`today`, `diagnostic`, `grammar`, `connectors`, `redemittel`, `wortschatz`, `case_trainer`, `learning_map`, `home`.
